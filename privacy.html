Privacy Policy – Vault-Zahlen

Last updated: 2026-11-16

1. What this app is

Vault-Zahlen is a private, read-only analytics tool. It is operated by the owner of the TikTok accounts it is connected to and is used only to view statistics of those same accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

2. Data we access

When the account owner authorizes the app, it reads only the following data from that account through the TikTok API: basic profile information (display name, avatar), account statistics (follower count, following count, likes count, video count) and the list of the account's public videos with their public counts (views, likes, comments, shares).

The app does not post, edit or delete any content and does not read private messages, contacts or other personal data.

3. How data is used and stored

The data is used only to create performance reports for the account owner. Reports and access tokens are stored locally on the account owner's own computer. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or used to train machine-learning models.

4. Retention and deletion

Stored data is kept only as long as needed for reporting. The account owner can delete all stored data and tokens at any time and can revoke the app's access in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy > Security & permissions > Apps and services. After revocation the app can no longer read any data.

5. Security

Access tokens are kept outside of any shared or public location and are not published.
